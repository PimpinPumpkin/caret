Caret is a free and open source keyboard that looks and feels like the one you are used to, without sending what you type anywhere.

<ul>
  <li>Swipe typing with a free library (the models are a one-time download, or can be imported from a file)</li>
  <li>Rounded keys, dynamic colors on Android 12 and newer, and custom themes</li>
  <li>A microphone key you can pin to the left or right and resize, which hands dictation to your voice input app</li>
  <li>Toolbar with pinned actions and an all-actions panel: clipboard history, undo, redo, cut, copy, paste, select, one-handed, split and floating modes</li>
  <li>Resize the keyboard by dragging, plus separate scales for keys, rows and fonts</li>
  <li>Hold or swipe backspace to delete whole words</li>
  <li>Emoji search, custom emoji font, number pad, custom layouts and multilingual typing</li>
  <li>Dictionaries for suggestions and spell check can be added per language</li>
  <li>Backup and restore of settings and learned words</li>
</ul>

The internet permission is used only for downloads you start yourself (swipe models, an emoji font).

Caret is a fork of HeliBoard, which is based on OpenBoard and the AOSP keyboard. Licensed under GPL-3.0.
